Our story

Ambition should not stop at a border.

Founders were not being slowed down by a lack of potential. They were being slowed down by not knowing what to do, when, where, and in what order.

These were the questions that kept coming back:

  • Should this be a corporation, an LLC, an LLP or a private limited company?
  • Is my intellectual property actually protected outside my home market?
  • Which jurisdiction makes the most tax sense, and what does it cost to be wrong?
  • How do I trade globally without triggering a permanent establishment?
  • Is this business GDPR-compliant, and what changes under the EU Data Protection Act?

They were not questions. They were roadblocks. So we built a support system rather than another consultancy: one that clears the fog, reduces friction, and lets builders move without second-guessing every step. Behind it sits a trusted ecosystem of StellarPartners who bring local expertise to a global strategy. Faster launches, stronger protection, smarter international scale.

StellarPartners

Independent professionals, separately engaged.

How it works

You engage StellarStart Global Limited. Where a matter needs a locally qualified professional (a filing agent, an admitted advocate, a licensed accountant), we can introduce or coordinate with them. The client separately engages them, and they control and are responsible for the regulated work.

What it means for you

One scope document, one fee estimate, one point of escalation. You are told which parts of the work sit with a partner firm and where the professional responsibility for that part rests.
